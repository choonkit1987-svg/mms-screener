What the 2-week model looks at

Weights learned from history. Positive = pushes the call towards "rise", negative = towards "fall". Re-trained every Sunday.

Agent Desk is a research tool, not financial advice. Its calls are probabilities learned from past prices and SEC filings; the test results above show how often they were right before, which does not guarantee the future. Fundamentals come from companies' 10-Q/10-K filings at data.sec.gov (Q4 is derived from the annual report); the next-report date is estimated as about 3 months after the last filing. Crypto has no financial reports, so coins are judged on price action and risk only.
