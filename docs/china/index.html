How the agents decide

1 · Stage agent

  • Daily chart, 150-day moving average only.
  • Stage 2: 150 MA rising, price above it. Further above = stronger.
  • Stage 4: 150 MA falling, price below it — the shorting stage.
  • Stage 1 / 3: 150 MA flat (base / top).
Course ch.1 · Stage 1–4

2 · Contraction agent

  • Counts pullbacks (C1, C2 …) from the highest high since the 150 MA crossed the 200 MA.
  • Each contraction must be shallower, each low higher.
  • Buy point in C2–C6, and the buying contraction must be under 10%.
  • Breakout zone inside C1: Pivot, Cheat or Low Cheat third.
Course ch.2 · Contraction, Base, Breakout zone

3 · Volume agent

  • Counts valid candles (body bigger than both wicks) whose volume beats the 50-day volume average.
  • More big up-days than big down-days favours a long; the reverse favours a short.
  • 50-day average volume × price must exceed US$1M a day.
Course ch.3 · Volume

4 · Risk agent

  • Entry: contraction high + 1% buffer (short: low − 1%).
  • Stop: contraction low − 1% (short: high + 1%).
  • Target 1:2. Move the stop to entry at 1:1.5. Trail the 50 MA ∓ 1% for passive profit.
  • First position 6.25% of capital; step up to 12.5% then 25% after wins.
Course ch.4–6 · Buffer, Stop loss, Breakeven, Amount

5 · Ranking agent

  • Score out of 100: Stage 25, Contraction 30, Trigger proximity 20, Volume 15, 6-month relative strength 10.
  • Only valid setups that have not run more than 5% past entry are ranked as picks.
  • If fewer than 5 qualify, the best remaining names fill the list, marked Watch with the reason they fail.
